Install

Add it to your home screen

Two minutes, no app store, no APK. Share the app link, open it in the browser below, done.

🍎 iPhone / iPad

  1. Open the app link in Safari (not other browsers).
  2. Tap the Share button ⎋ at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add — then open PW Budget from your home screen.
  5. It now runs as its own app, fully offline. 🎉

🤖 Android

  1. Open the app link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Install — an app icon appears with its own window.
  5. From then on it runs offline, even in airplane mode. ✈️
💡 First-time tip: open the app once on Wi-Fi so it saves itself to your phone. After that — purchases, credits, budgets, analytics and the calculator all work with no internet at all. Your data stays on your phone.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. After the one-time install over Wi-Fi, the entire app runs on your phone. Purchases, credits, budgets and analytics all work in airplane mode. Internet is only needed the first time, and briefly for updates.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in the browser's local storage. It is never uploaded anywhere. That also means your data doesn't sync between devices — each phone keeps its own copy.

How much does it cost?

The app is free during early access. The planned price is a one-time GH₵100 — no subscription — which will include all future updates.

Do I need an app store?

No. iPhone installs via Safari's "Add to Home Screen", Android via Chrome's "Install app". No APK, no Play Store, no review waiting.

How do updates work?

When a new version is released and your phone is online, the app shows a "New version available — tap to update" banner. One tap reloads the new version. Your data is untouched.

Can I use it for multiple shops?

Yes — add every shop or plant as a branch, and scope the dashboard, purchases, credits and analytics to each one.
