Our story

A Greek word, an ancient idea

Praxis (πρᾶξις) is the ancient Greek word for doing — action as opposed to theory. It shares its roots with the very idea of practice: the bridge between a thought and a result.

The word travelled through Alexandria — the ancient world's great meeting place of knowledge and craft — where thinking and making were never separate. That is the spirit PRAXIS carries into software: knowledge is only worth what it produces.

So our name is a promise. We don't ship theories — we ship things that work: for the print shop owner checking credits with no data bundle, for the business that needs answers today, not demos tomorrow.
